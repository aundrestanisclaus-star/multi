    • Welcome to Grenada United FC Football Club

    Founded in 2026, Grenada United FC is a community-focused football club dedicated to bringing players, supporters, and families together through the love of the game.

    Our club provides opportunities for players of different ages and skill levels to develop their football abilities, build confidence, and enjoy being part of a team.

    Our Mission

    Our mission is to promote football, teamwork, discipline, and respect both on and off the pitch. We believe football is more than just a sport—it is a way to build friendships, strengthen our community, and inspire the next generation.

    Our Values

    Teamwork — We succeed together.

    Respect — We respect our teammates, opponents, coaches, and supporters.

    Development — We encourage every player to improve and reach their potential.

    Community — We are proud to represent and support our local community.

    Passion — We bring energy and commitment to every match.

    Together on the pitch. Together as a club.
