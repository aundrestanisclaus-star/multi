    • About Us

    Grenada United FC, proudly known as The Spice Warriors, is a football club built on passion, teamwork, courage, and determination. Our club represents the fighting spirit of Grenada and the love that our community has for the beautiful game.

    We believe football is more than just winning matches. It is about bringing people together, developing young talent, building confidence, and creating a strong sense of community. Every player who puts on the Grenada United jersey is expected to give their best, support their teammates, and play with pride.

    As The Spice Warriors, we step onto the pitch with one goal: to compete with strength, respect, and an unstoppable warrior spirit.
